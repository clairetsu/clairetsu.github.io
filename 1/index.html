01   Images of the Russian Empire

CS180: Intro to Computer Vision and Computational Photography

Overview

Prokudin-Gorskii photographed each scene three times through a red, a green, and a blue filter, recording all three exposures onto a single glass plate. The digitized scans are three stacked grayscale images. Stacking them naively into an RGB image doesn't work, because the camera shifted slightly between exposures. This project recovers the color photograph by searching for the translation that best aligns the green and red plates onto the blue one.
